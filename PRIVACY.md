# Privacy Policy

Zero is open-source software maintained under [Simpl](https://simpl.global) and licensed under [AGPL-3.0](LICENSE).

- **No identifiers or accounts:** Zero does not collect phone numbers, emails, usernames, or social graphs.
- **Post-quantum end-to-end encryption:** Messages and files stay encrypted client-side (`sntrup761` + `X448` Double Ratchet, 16 KB padded blocks) inside encrypted SQLCipher local storage.
- **Stateless zero-log relays:** Messages are buffered encrypted only until delivery or expiry without access logs.

For full technical and architectural details, read [`README.md`](README.md), [`WHITEPAPER.md`](WHITEPAPER.md), or visit [simpl.global](https://simpl.global). Contact: `support@simpl.global`.
